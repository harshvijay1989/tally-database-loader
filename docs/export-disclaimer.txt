IMPORTANT NOTE — TALLY DATA EXPORT
Please read before running a full data export.

WHAT THIS TOOL DOES
The connector reads your Tally data and exports it — including every available
field of every record — into CSV files (and on to your chosen destination),
giving you a complete extract of your Tally information.

THE TECHNICAL REALITY
Tally's built-in HTTP/XML gateway is single-threaded and was designed for
targeted, on-demand lookups — not for exporting all fields of all records at
once. Pulling a complete dataset pushes Tally beyond its normal request pattern.
Because of this, on larger companies Tally may temporarily become slow, stop
responding, or appear "stuck" while an export is running. This is a
characteristic of how Tally itself is built, not a fault in your data.

WHAT WE HAVE DONE ABOUT IT
- Data is fetched in small, bounded batches (never one giant request).
- Requests are sent one at a time (never overlapping).
- Automatic retries and adaptive splitting handle heavy batches.
- Every run is logged in detail so issues can be diagnosed quickly.
This is dramatically safer than a naive "export all," but because the underlying
limitation is in Tally, we cannot guarantee it will never occur.

HOW IT INTERACTS WITH TALLY
The connector only reads from Tally — it never writes to, edits, or deletes
anything. In normal operation, if Tally becomes unresponsive you can restart
Tally Prime, reopen the company, and re-run the export.

PLEASE BACK UP YOUR DATA FIRST
Because a full export drives Tally harder than everyday use, please take a
current backup of your Tally company data before running an export.

DISCLAIMER OF LIABILITY
You use this tool and run exports at your own risk. While we have taken
significant measures to make the process as safe as possible, the underlying
limitation lies within Tally's own export gateway and is outside our control.
Accordingly, we do not accept any responsibility or liability for any data loss,
data corruption, downtime, or business disruption arising from the use of this
connector or from Tally becoming unresponsive during an export. It is your
responsibility to maintain adequate backups.

RECOMMENDATIONS
- Take a backup before running a large export.
- Run large exports when Tally is not being actively used.
- Keep the company open in Tally with its HTTP server enabled (port 9000).
- Prefer running big, first-time exports during off-peak hours.

If you run into any issue, share the connector's log file (sync-log.txt) with
us — it records exactly what happened and where, so we can help quickly.
